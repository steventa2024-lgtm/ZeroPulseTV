## Supplied Liquid Glass wallpapers (lg01-lg05)

Five wallpapers supplied by the owner of this launcher for the Liquid Glass Home. They are bundled **byte for byte as supplied**
(no recompression, no resizing; `VisualPolishTests` checks each file's SHA-256), and are the Liquid Glass collection. No third-party
credit is recorded for them. Their authorship and licence were not stated when they were supplied (the file name of one,
`Gemini_Generated_Image_...`, suggests it was produced with an image generator), so **confirming that you may redistribute them is
the owner's to do** before any public release of this APK. Four are 1024 x 572 pixels and are enlarged on a 1080p or 4K screen; one
is 2752 x 1536.

reasonable to the medium. That is the reading this build relies on; it is not legal advice.
